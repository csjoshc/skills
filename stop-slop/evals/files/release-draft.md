# Moving the Orchard app to weekly releases

Last quarter we shipped once a month, and every release was a scramble. Nobody wanted to
touch the build on a Thursday.

We decided to leverage a robust release train — a fixed Tuesday cut — to streamline the
process. It's not about speed, it's about predictability. Honestly, the old way was
broken, and we really needed to delve into why.

Now a Tuesday cut goes out whether or not the big feature is ready. Mina Okafor, who runs
QA, says the regression suite takes 40 minutes, so we gate on it.

This is huge for the team. Let that sink in.
