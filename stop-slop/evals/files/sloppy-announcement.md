# Why we moved to weekly releases

Here's the thing: release cadence is a multifaceted challenge. It turns out that shipping
every week genuinely transforms how a team works. The reasons are structural.

Our tool, Larkspur, serves as a centralized hub for scheduling. It isn't just a calendar,
it's a game changer. Experts agree that frequent releases foster trust. The result?
Faster. Safer. Better.

In conclusion, weekly releases mark a pivotal moment for the team, highlighting our
commitment to quality.
